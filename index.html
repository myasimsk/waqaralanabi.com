Returns & exchanges

If you need a different size, you can request an exchange subject to stock. The item must be unused and unwashed, with all original tags still attached. The customer pays the exchange shipping charges.

  1. Message the official Waqar Al Anabi WhatsApp with your order number, the item, and the size you need.
  2. The WA team will review your request, confirm stock and shipping charges, and create the replacement order after approving the exchange.
  3. When the courier delivers your replacement, hand them your original parcel and receive the new one.
